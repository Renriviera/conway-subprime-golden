**Manuscript:** Golden-ratio growth of Conway's subprime closure

Dear Editors,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript for publication in the *Journal of Number Theory*.

Starting from \(1\) and closing under Conway's subprime map \((a,b)\mapsto s(a+b)\) produces finite generations \(C_n\subset\mathbb N\). Caragiu, Vicol, and Zaki proved that these generations exhaust \(\mathbb N\), and conjectured that the cardinalities grow by the golden ratio. The manuscript proves that conjecture, in the sharper form \(M_n\sim Q_n\sim c\varphi^n\) and \(|C_n|\sim c\varphi^{n-1}\). The argument combines their exhaustion theorem with an almost-all Goldbach theorem for nearly equal primes and a restricted binary representation lemma, proved in the appendix by the Hardy–Littlewood circle method.

The same limit is formally verified in Lean 4, taking as analytic input the Siegel–Walfisz theorem and a Vinogradov-type minor-arc bound. The Lean sources are included with the arXiv posting and at the GitHub repository cited in the paper.

The work is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and (once the arXiv identifier is assigned) will be posted as a preprint on arXiv under `math.NT`. Declarations of interest: none.
